## 2. AI Photoshoot & Commercial Photography

### Overview
An AI Photoshoot by creo STUDIO generates ultra-realistic, studio-grade fashion and commercial product photography using custom generative models and virtual lighting setups. It transforms raw flat-lay photos or basic catalog images into campaign-ready editorial visuals without physical studios or models.

### Key Deliverables
- **Zero Logistical Sets**: No studio bookings, travel overhead, equipment rental, or weather dependencies.
- **Custom Brand Models**: Training tailored virtual talent matching precise demographic, aesthetic, and posture requirements.
- **Material Fidelity**: Realistic cloth draping, true fabric weave texture, ambient occlusion, and accurate skin pores.
- **Platform-Ready Angles**: Generates front, back, 3/4 angle, and macro-detail padded shots from single source images.
- **Full Commercial Licensing**: Complete ownership of all generated visual variants for web, social, and print.

### Key Metrics
- **Cost Reduction**: Up to 70% lower budget compared to traditional physical studio setups.
- **Turnaround Time**: Initial visual sets delivered in 24 to 48 hours.
- **Resolution**: 2K to 4K ultra-high-definition exports suitable for e-commerce zooms and high-res print catalogs.

### What is an AI photoshoot?
An AI photoshoot is a digital production process that creates hyper-realistic commercial and fashion photography using generative AI models. It eliminates physical sets, lighting crews, and model bookings by generating studio-quality lighting, realistic fabric textures, and customized virtual models directly from product reference images.

creo STUDIO builds custom web applications using React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. Each website is engineered with semantic HTML, fluid motion animations, AI-integrated backend endpoints (like Google Gemini), and built-in search engine and answer engine optimization (GEO/AEO).

Clients of creo STUDIO receive 100% full commercial rights and intellectual property ownership for all custom web code, AI photoshoots, generated video clips, and catalog layouts upon delivery.

### What are the turnaround times for projects?
AI photoshoot suites are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Custom web production sprints range from 1 to 3 weeks depending on technical specifications, backend integrations, and content scope.
